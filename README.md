## `01 / THE ENGINEER`

I build the invisible systems that make digital products work.

I'm a backend-focused software professional with **5+ years of experience** in software development, cloud-based applications, and API-driven systems. My work revolves around turning business requirements into reliable backend services, designing clean integrations, and building systems that are easier to maintain and evolve.

Currently, I'm a **Product Technical Analyst at EdgeVerve Systems**, working in the banking software domain, including core payment systems.

I care about more than making code work. I care about why it works, how it behaves under pressure, and how it will be maintained years from now.

<details>
<summary><b>🧭 My engineering philosophy</b></summary>

- **Design for change:** Build components that can evolve without rewriting everything.
- **Make reliability a feature:** Predictable behavior, meaningful errors, and thoughtful failure handling matter.
- **Keep it simple:** The best solution is often the simplest one that meets the real requirements.
- **Automate the repeatable:** Use tooling and CI/CD to make delivery more consistent.
- **Understand the system:** Good backend engineering means understanding data, dependencies, infrastructure, and business logic.

</details>
